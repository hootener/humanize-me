# Context

The taxonomy had nine types with no grouping, which meant an agent placing a
new node had nine coin-flips and no model to make them with. Three symptoms,
all measured in this repository:

- `goal` had a template, a folder, an index, a linter type and a graph colour,
  and **no skill anywhere wrote one**. The top of the graph was decorative.
- `context` was carrying the brief, stack facts, running findings and review
  outcomes at once — so coarse that `bin/relevant` and `bin/ancestry` both
  invent a fake `experiment` kind by sniffing for `/experiments/` in the path,
  and both sort it *above* decision as "the most load-bearing entries".
- `initiative-pointer` had zero instances and was named nowhere outside the
  linter, duplicating what a plan's own `description` already says.

# Decision

**Four categories**, left to right:

| Category | Types | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| Intent | `goal`, `decision`, `principle` | what we are trying to do, and what we have settled |
| Approach | `plan`, `spec` | how we mean to do it |
| Action | `task` | what gets done |
| Evidence | `evidence` | what we observed, and what it corrects |

`glossary` sits outside the four: it is definitional, not a claim about the
work.

**`context` becomes `evidence`.** The brief is evidence that was given rather
than gathered, marked with `resource:`. `initiative-pointer` is deleted.
Taxonomy goes from nine types to seven plus the glossary.

**A retro is not a fifth category.** It is an Evidence node whose edges point
back up: `amends:` names what it corrects, `reviewed:` names what it covered.
A measurement is the same type with neither edge. The distinction is carried
by structure, not by a subtype field.

**`spec` is Approach, not Action.** The boundary is *is this executable* — a
spec says what will exist, a plan says in what order, and only a task is done.

**The direction rule is temporal, not spatial.** A node may depend only on
nodes that were already settled when it was written. Intent → Approach →
Action is the common case because intent usually precedes approach, but a goal
written today may legitimately cite evidence from a plan that shipped last
quarter. What is forbidden is editing that plan to cite the new goal, because
a reader of the plan would then see a justification that did not exist when it
was written.

The loop therefore closes **through Evidence**: a new goal cites what a
previous effort *established*, not the plan that established it. If no such
Evidence node exists, that is the finding — the outcome was never recorded —
and writing it is the fix.

Two edges deliberately violate the rule and are the exceptions that make it
useful: `superseded_by` and `amends` point forward in time, because a reader
who has reached a stale node must be carried to what replaced it.

# Alternatives

- **Three categories (Intent, Approach, Action)** — rejected: leaves
  measurement and the brief homeless, and leaves the graph a one-way pipeline
  with no way for what was learned to correct what was intended. The tooling
  had already worked around the gap by inventing `experiment` from a path.
- **Retro as a fifth category** — rejected: a retro has no region of its own;
  it is defined entirely by what it points at. Giving it a category would put
  one node type in two places.
- **`subtype:` on evidence** — rejected: a type system inside a field. The
  edges already distinguish them and are enumerable.
- **Spatial direction rule ("Action may cite Intent, never the reverse")** —
  rejected: it forbids a legitimate and common case, a new goal justified by
  what an earlier effort proved. Time is the thing actually being protected.

# Consequences

- `bin/lint` enforces the seven types and errors on `context` and
  `initiative-pointer`.
- `bin/lint` can check the direction rule against `timestamp`, catching gross
  violations; same-day ones are invisible, since timestamps are dates.
- `bin/relevant` and `bin/ancestry` drop the `/experiments/` path sniff.
- `bin/graph-build` gains an `evidence` fill and loses two.
- The README's diagram (`docs/lineage.svg`) is an artifact of this decision and
  is regenerated by `bin/diagram` when the categories change.
