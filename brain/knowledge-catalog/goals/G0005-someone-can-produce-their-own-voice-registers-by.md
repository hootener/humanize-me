# Goal

A person who has never met this project clones it, runs a guided process against
their own writing, and ends with three voice registers tuned to them — theirs to
copy into whatever project they like.

This is a **second product with a second audience**, alongside the library in
[G0002](G0002-humanize-me-installs-into-another-project-in-one.md). The library
is installed and gives an agent generic craft. The workshop is cloned and
produces something personal.

# Why

**The method is the part of this project with evidence behind it.** Almost
nothing else is.

Two runs of it so far. A defect harvest over eight passages produced eleven
usable rules where nine ranking trials had produced two, with no retractions
([E0011](../reference/E0011-eight-defect-harvest-passages-produced-nine-rules-where.md)).
A paragraph-by-paragraph review of a generated document produced roughly fifteen
more. And the one unambiguous success measured in this project is a rule from
that process reaching a writer who had never seen the operator's feedback: he
named a missing section, the rule went into the craft skill, and a fresh writer
produced the section he then endorsed without being told any of it.

Against that, the artifacts have gone badly. A described profile beat a
no-guidance control 3/3 on headings and **0/6 on prose**
([E0009](../reference/E0009-the-profile-works-on-headings-and-not-on.md)). One
of its markers was measured as actively harmful and withdrawn
([E0010](../reference/E0010-a-pooled-marker-floor-pushed-output-away-from.md)).
The operator identified his own published writing at chance.

So the project has been shipping the output and keeping the process to itself,
and the process is the half that works.

**It also fixes who the operator is.** [D0012](../decisions/D0012-make-it-adoptable-first-then-dogfood-it-as.md)
asked that the operator's own setup be built the way an end user would build it.
Today it was built by hand, in conversation, through paths no adopter has. A
workshop is what makes D0012 true rather than aspirational: the operator becomes
its first user and every rough edge costs him first.

# Signals

- Someone who did not write this repository clones it, follows the process, and
  leaves with registers they would actually use — without reading how the
  library is built.
- The rules a person names about their own writing end up in their own profile
  without anyone hand-editing a file for them.
- The operator's next register — casual or research — is built by running the
  workshop rather than by a conversation like the one that built the
  professional one.
- A rule named once by a user does not have to be named twice.
