The check [D0018](../decisions/D0018-a-voice-profile-states-its-claims-checkably.md)
designed and nothing had run. Six blind trials, one operator, 2026-11-10.

# Setup

Two Codecov articles the generating agents had never seen, written by the
operator in January 2023. Content-only briefs by a separate agent — every fact,
number and product term, in order, **no headings and no phrasings**, verified
mechanically (the first attempt leaked 10 of 13 headings and was rewritten).

Three arms, generated by agents with no exposure to the operator's corpus:

| arm | input |
|---|---|
| control | the brief alone |
| profile | the brief + `voice-professional/SKILL.md` + `professional.md` — what ships |
| real | what the operator published |

Six trials: headings-only, opening ~90 words, closing ~90 words, per article.
Each shuffled independently. Markup normalised across arms. The operator ranked
each trial *mine / second / worst* with the key sealed until all six were in.

# Result

| arm | mean rank | ranks |
|---|---|---|
| control | **1.83** | 3, 1, 1, 3, 1, 2 |
| profile | 2.00 | 2, 2, 2, 1, 2, 3 |
| the operator's own writing | **2.17** | 1, 3, 3, 2, 3, 1 |

- **The profile ranked above the control in 2 of 6 trials.** Two-sided exact
  binomial p = 0.688. No detectable difference, and the direction favours the
  arm that received no style guidance at all.
- **The operator identified his own writing in 2 of 6 trials.** Chance is 2 of
  6.
- **His own published prose ranked worst in 3 of 6 trials** and carried the
  worst mean rank of the three arms.

# The one pattern that separated

| trial type | identified own | rank of his real text | mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| headings (n = 2) | 1/2 | 1st, 2nd | **1.50** |
| prose excerpts (n = 4) | 1/4 | 3rd, 3rd, 3rd, 1st | **2.50** |

On headings he placed his own writing first and second. On 90-word prose
excerpts he ranked it last three times in four.

This agrees with [E0006](E0006-the-operator-rejected-a-profile-that-passed-every.md),
where the rejection of a generated document was delivered as a complaint about
a heading. The working hypothesis it supports: **heading style is legible to
this author as his own; prose rhythm at excerpt scale is not.** If that holds,
"it sounds like you doing an impression of me" was substantially a heading
judgment, and the project has been weighting the wrong signal.

Two unprompted reasons he gave are worth more than the rankings, because both
name something no marker measures:

- A version placed worst "primarily because the bullet points are very long".
  The profile has a bullet *share* marker and nothing about bullet *length*.
- A heading set placed worst on one heading, "Where to install it and what you
  need to do" — which names two subjects joined by *and*. Every heading in his
  corpus names one. The profile requires a heading to name its subject and does
  not require it to name only one.

# What this does and does not support

**It cannot show the profile is useless.** With n = 6 and a null of 0.5, a clean
6/6 sweep is the *minimum* for one-sided p < 0.05. This run has no power to
detect a modest effect. It rules out a large one.

**It does undermine the instrument.** The operator ranked his own writing below
generated text more often than not, which means the question he was answering
was not purely "which is mine". [E0007](E0007-state-of-the-art-for-reproducing-a-specific.md)
predicted exactly this from outside: author self-judgment correlates with the
best authorship metric at r = 0.244 (n = 81, pre-registered), and in the same
study participants rated post-edited text as *no less* representative of their
own style than their unassisted writing (p = .9062). This session reproduced
that finding on a sample of one.

Confounds named rather than waved off: the test articles are three years old and
the voice may have moved; the three trials within an article are not
independent, since recognising the content in an opening carries into the
closing; excerpts discard whole-document structure, which is part of why the
headings trial exists separately.

# Consequences

- No evidence supports [D0013](../decisions/D0013-the-craft-ships-generic-and-the-voice-is.md)'s
  assumption that a generated *description* is the right artifact. The decision
  is not overturned on n = 6, but it is now unsupported rather than merely
  untested, and [T0017](../../tasks/voice/T0017-experiment-population-matched-exemplar-retrieval-versus-a-described.md)
  becomes the more urgent experiment.
- Blind operator judgment is not a sufficient primary instrument for prose. It
  retains value for headings, where it discriminated.
- Two candidate markers arrived free: maximum bullet length, and one subject per
  heading.
