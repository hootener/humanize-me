The fourth defect harvest under
[D0022](../decisions/D0022-the-preference-signal-is-named-defects-never-blind.md),
and the first on the precise register. Six passages, 892 words, generated by an
agent reading `voice-precise` plus `~/.claude/voice/profile/precise.md`, judged
one at a time with the single question *what is wrong with this one?*

# Yield

| harvest | passages | rules | clean passages | rules per passage |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| professional | 8 | 11 | — | 1.4 |
| casual round 1 | 6 | 28 | 0 | 4.7 |
| casual round 2 | 6 | 25 | 0 | 4.2 |
| **precise** | **6** | **11** | **2** | **1.8** |

The lowest defect rate of the four, and the first harvest to produce a clean
passage at all — two of them, P1 and P6. The precise profile was also the
thinnest of the three when it went in (1,142 words against casual's 1,966 and
professional's 3,379), so the result is not explained by the profile having
more to say. A plausible reading is that the register itself is narrower: fewer
moves are available, so there are fewer ways to get it wrong.

That reading is a hypothesis, not a finding. One harvest on one register
separates it from the alternative — that the generating agent happened to do
well — by nothing.

# The two rules that contradict the profile

Nine of the eleven rules add to the profile. Two correct it, and both were
surfaced by the operator rewriting a line rather than by rejecting one.

**Rates are register-specific.** Two constructions were rejected as "too
flowery", with a request for "a much more analytical straightforward way".
Both are recorded professional moves: the balanced antithesis, and the
aphoristic short landing, which carries three confirmations and is among the
best-attested rules in the record. The rule survived into precise; its rate did
not. Written up as
[D0028](../decisions/D0028-a-move-s-rate-is-set-per-register-not.md).

**The impersonal construction is right for the generalisable finding.** The
profile was built on a split — voice from a first-person post-mortem, evidence
discipline from academic papers — and instructed the writer to discard the
academic register's impersonality wholesale. The operator then rewrote a
generated line to be *more* impersonal than it was:

> Better signal is obtained by asking the reader to pick out defects in the
> prose, because this task can be performed independent of preference.

and called it "a more professional precise way to say it". The split was drawn
on the wrong axis. It is not corpus against corpus but sentence against
sentence: first person for owning an error or a judgment, impersonal for a
finding that generalises beyond the writer.

# Three rules about order, not wording

Unusually for a harvest, three of the eleven concern what comes before what:
method and controls before results, define the term before the finding,
enumerate in parallel before judging. Taken together they say this register's
characteristic failure is sequence rather than diction — a reader given a
number before the method that produced it cannot weigh it, whatever words
surround it.

`define-the-term-before-the-finding` is the fourth confirmation of a rule
already in `common.md` and the first in this register. It is sharper here
because the term *is* the measurement: a reader who does not know what a
contraction rate counts cannot evaluate any figure that follows.

# The batch review ran and worked

[D0026](../decisions/D0026-every-move-in-a-profile-carries-a-frequency.md)
added a step requiring the generating agent to review a batch as a set before
delivering it, so that rationed effects are spent once across the batch rather
than once per passage. This was its first run.

Each rationed effect appeared exactly once: the short landing in P2 only, the
quoted past-voice question in P3 only, the conceded counter-argument in P5
only. The step also caught two trailing prepositions, a bare demonstrative and
an unsupported quantifier before delivery.

**One run, self-reported by the agent that performed it.** The effect
distribution is checkable in the passages; the claim that the step caused it is
not, since no un-reviewed batch was generated for comparison.
