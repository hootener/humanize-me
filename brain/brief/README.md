# brief

**Evidence, given rather than gathered.** Source material the project answers, as received.
Verbatim — not analysis. Analysis lives in the knowledge catalog and links back here.

A brief is an `evidence` node with a `resource:` and no `amends:` or `reviewed:` — the three
edges are what distinguish what was given, what was measured, and what a review corrected.
