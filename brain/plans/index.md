# Plans

How things get built: implementation plans, sequencing, and the reasoning
behind an approach. A plan carries `serves:` pointing at the goal it pursues.

Plans are **not** decisions. A decision says what we chose and why; a plan
says how we intend to carry it out, and is superseded by what actually
shipped rather than deleted, so the gap between intent and outcome stays
visible.
