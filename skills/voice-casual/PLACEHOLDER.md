# voice-casual — not yet written

The register is decided; the craft is not.

**A claim here rests on the writer's say-so.** No reasoning is owed. That is
what separates it from `voice-professional`, where the reader is owed why.

This directory holds no `SKILL.md` on purpose. A stub skill that loaded and
produced nothing useful would be worse than an absent one, so this register is
left out of `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` until the craft exists. Adding it is
one file plus one line in the manifest.

Written after the professional register settles the shape — see
`brain/tasks/voice/`.

## Inherited before it is written

[D0020](../../brain/knowledge-catalog/decisions/D0020-craft-governs-ownership-and-falsifiability-a-profile-governs.md)
applies to this register too. The craft here may require that a judgment has a
holder and that a confidence is something the writer could be wrong about. It
may not legislate how much the writer hedges or which words they hedge with —
that is voice, and the profile governs it.
