# voice-research — not yet written

The register is decided; the craft is not.

**A claim here rests on evidence outside the writer, cited.** The writer's
judgment is not sufficient warrant, which is what separates it from
`voice-professional`.

The name is about warrant, not subject: an incident post-mortem or a pricing
analysis belongs here, and a paper on a topic you are guessing about does not.

This directory holds no `SKILL.md` on purpose, for the reason given in
`skills/voice-casual/PLACEHOLDER.md`. Adding it is one file plus one line in
`.claude-plugin/plugin.json`.

Written after the professional register settles the shape — see
`brain/tasks/voice/`.

## Inherited before it is written

[D0020](../../brain/knowledge-catalog/decisions/D0020-craft-governs-ownership-and-falsifiability-a-profile-governs.md)
applies to this register too. The craft here may require that a judgment has a
holder and that a confidence is something the writer could be wrong about. It
may not legislate how much the writer hedges or which words they hedge with —
that is voice, and the profile governs it.
